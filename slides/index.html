Why not recall, as we proposed

Recall counts a 5 USD fraud and a 1,500 USD fraud the same. The loss doesn't.

A window

1,000 payments in a row. Every setup decides the same windows, so differences are paired.

We report customers as well as dollars: a cheaper setup can still decline more good customers.

They stack into one number, the mean cost per window. This is a change from the proposal, so say it out loud: we promised recall at the same precision. We moved to dollars because recall treats every fraud alike and the loss doesn't. We still report frauds stopped and good customers declined next to every cost. BThe ruleEarlier test

Weigh the amount in each decision, and the cheapest option changes

Worked examples, expected cost in USDapprovereviewdeclinecheapest

Approve: p × amount. Decline: (1 − p) × friction, where friction is 10% of the amount, at least 1 USD; the customer line adds a flat 10 USD to the friction. Review: 10 + λ {{cost.lambda|num}}. p is fitted on dev windows and used only to weigh costs.

Same logreg scores, Phase 14: two lines {{rule.phase14.lines|int}} → rule {{rule.phase14.rule|int}}, {{rule.phase14.diff.mean|signed}}, CI {{rule.phase14.diff.ci|ci}}, {{rule.phase14.diff|dist}}. But good customers declined rise, {{manual.phase14.good_declined_lines|int}} → {{manual.phase14.good_declined_rule|int}}.

[Swan · 1:00] Our proposal had two fixed cut-offs on the score. They ignore the amount. This is arithmetic, there is no model on this slide. p is a number fitted on dev windows; we use it only to weigh costs. It is not a probability of fraud. Start: 30 USD, p 0.02: approving is cheapest, 0.60 against 2.94 and 15. Click: same p, 1,500 USD: now a review is cheapest, 15 against 30 and 147, because the amount alone changed the sum. Click: 30 USD, p 0.2: decline, 2.40 against 6. Click: the customer line adds a flat 10 USD to the friction of a decline, so it becomes 0.8 × 13 = 10.40, and it flips to approve. If asked what lambda is: the price of using up one of the reviews, since at most 5% of a window can be reviewed. It is picked on dev windows. Drag the sliders if the room wants to try a case. The rule was the biggest dollar gain we measured: on the same logreg scores, 825 USD per window, distinguishable. It also declined more good customers, 982 against 655, and we report both.
