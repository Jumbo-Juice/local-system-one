# The story (story/index.html)

Scope: one scroll page, the story of the whole project's findings. Mode: Read.

Audience and job: a newcomer reading alone (a classmate, a grader, a curious visitor who missed the
talk) who wants to answer "Can a model that never writes be trusted to decide?" with the evidence in
hand. Content: six trials, each ending on a stamp (yes, no, only with help, can't tell), then the
tally, the limits and the sources. Two things to try (the order flip, the rule explorer) and two
replays from pinned runs. Constraints: no model runs, no IEEE-CIS row, numbers bound to data.js,
no network, not a sales page, not a chopped-up deck, not a lab notebook.

Chosen direction: the category standard, picked by the owner on the decision page (2026-11-12,
after a safer re-roll). Craft bar named by the owner: Distill and The Pudding. Memorable moment:
the stamp landing at the end of each trial and taking its place in the tally.

Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The scrolling explainer as Distill and The Pudding make it, played straight: one question,
six trials, a figure that stays put while the text passes it. It refuses the dark AI landing page
with glowing charts and a hero metric.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, near-black ink, hairline grey rules. One blue that only ever means the
model itself (its lean, its time, its cost) or something the reader can touch. Four stamp colours, each with its own drawn mark
(green tick, red cross, amber plus, slate question mark). Figtree throughout, heavy for headings,
tabular numerals. Figures are flat SVG with direct labels: no card shadows, no gradients.

STORY: The reader learns what one look is, watches six trials, believes each stamp because the
evidence was on screen before it, and leaves with one sentence: quick enough, but every gain came
from the structure around the model or from training it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim bar: the project name left, four plain links right, a thin blue progress
line under it. Left, a 680 px column: the question as the heading, about 64 px, weight 800, then a
two-paragraph opening. Right, the one-look figure: a question, five lettered options with bars, the
leaned-to option in blue, a one-line caption. Below the column, the way forward: Trial 1. No label
above the heading.

FORM: The category standard (the standing exit), chosen by the owner. seed:cad619f72fa30b52.
Signature interaction: the stamp. Motion grammar: scroll steps change the pinned figure, replays
scrub with the scroll, one exponential ease-out; reduced motion shows final states.
